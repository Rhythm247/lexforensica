Case development timeline: electronic evidence

2014: Anvar P.V. v. P.K. Basheer. A certificate is mandatory for electronic records.
2020: Arjun Panditrao Khotkar. Reaffirms the certificate requirement, clarifies when it may be produced.
2023: BSA replaces the Evidence Act; section 63 carries the rule forward.
